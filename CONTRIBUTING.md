# Contributing to UGLY-SPACE

This repository is AI-generated academic satire produced at a human's
direction. Contributions should preserve that disclosure and the distinction
between mathematical results and comic interpretation.

- State assumptions and quantifiers before a theorem, and include its proof.
  Label incomplete arguments as conjectures or open questions.
- Do not describe numerical experiments as proofs, or the finite toy model
  as an empirical model of humans, AI systems, or employment.
- Verify references against a primary source. Never invent publications,
  quotations, authors, affiliations, reviews, or endorsements.
- Disclose substantial AI assistance in a contribution's description. Do not
  imply that the current manuscript has received human mathematical review.
- Keep the example reproducible. After changing the model, run `make tables`,
  `make check`, and `make paper`; explain any changes to the assumptions.

Useful extensions include adaptive search, sampling without replacement,
verification costs, or an explicit experimental design for testing the
Aesthetic Regularization Hypothesis. A theorem explaining why the current
paper is beautiful would require unusually strong evidence.
